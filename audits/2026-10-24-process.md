### F1 — The LD chat is the largest cost, and is not in the ledger. (A)

- `LEDGER.md:135`: "The lead designer chat's own cost … is **not in this ledger** (#270 (7))".
- Task figures: 389.7 USD for 2026-10-21..24 across six chats. Sessions in the same window:
  `STATUS.md` "about 304 USD" for 10-21/23 plus ≈ 13 USD of 10-24 sessions (PR #96 6.84,

  the window, ≈ 1.2× all sessions together. Per engineer PR in the window (13 PRs in the ledger)
  ≈ **30 USD of chat** against ≈ 25 USD of sessions.
- Every "cost" row, factor and 20 USD guideline (#191, `CLAUDE.md`) reasons about sessions only. The
  per-level total the owner sees is therefore the minority of the per-level spend.
- One model switch is reported to have cost 69.9 USD (18 % of chat spend). I cannot say which
  chat or why; the likely mechanism (a new model re-reads the whole context uncached) is
  *not verified*.
- Proposed resolution: put the chat's `cost_usd` in `LEDGER.md` per block; do not switch model
  inside a running chat — hand over at the boundary instead.
