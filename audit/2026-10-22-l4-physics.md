## 1. Armour plates (§13l.1, #255, #256, #258)

**Verdict: one law, not two.** Recomputed (T1, `a1.mjs`): c = 1545.364 m/s, plate σ = 0.2 × 7850 = 1570 kg/m², r = c σ = 2.4262e6 (kg·m/s per m²), exactly the cell's formula (`rooms.js:303-306` `entryArealDensity` → `fireRound` r = c × σ × chord, `rooms.js:408-409`). The M round: m = 6 kg, A = 3.0609e-3 m² (D = 62.4 mm, L = 249.7 mm), s₀ = m v / A = 1.5682e6, strength S = 3.0293e6. s₀/r = 0.646 < 1: **the 0.2 m plate stops the round**; it takes a = s₀ and loses a / r / `hitsToWreck` = 0.21545 (the literal 0.215 reproduced). That is the same line as a cell's (`rooms.js:415` `losses`). The beam burns the plate by the same law as a cell: 3000 J/kg × 39 250 kg = 117.75 MJ = 23.55 s at 5 MW (reproduced; the step 1413 of §13l.1 is the same number on the step grid). Deposit uses σ (Bethe) and heat goes to the mount room. Plates on walls only: nothing in the kernel can meet a deck plate, agreed.

### L1 (L, documented) A damaged plate or cell resists, deposits and weighs as if intact
- `js/rooms.js:275-276`: `cellArealDensity` = `destroyed ? 0 : cellMassOf / edge²`, integrity is not read; `entryArealDensity` for a plate likewise.
- `DESIGN.md:598` "Partial kinetic damage": a round "removes that cell's resistance R ... and takes 1 / hitsToWreck of the cell's integrity". Against `DESIGN.md` §13l.1 "Blast capacity from remaining integrity (#216 (3)... L4a)": blast reads integrity, kinetic and beam depth do not.
- Effect: a plate at integrity 0.14 (after four stopped rounds) resists a fifth round at full r = 2.426e6. Two channels disagree on what a damaged cell is.
- Proposed: either write "resistance is all or nothing" as the D pass's abstraction (item 34's pass should say so), or let σ follow remaining integrity (see U1: the physical reading, crater depth, does exactly that for a plate).

### L5 (L, minor) Cells clip an oblique path at the edge; plates do not
- `js/battle.js:262`: `chord = Math.min(1, (t1 - t0) / e)`; `rooms.js:303-306`: plate σ / cos θ, capped at edge (cos θ ≥ d / edge).
- `a6.mjs`: a 45° line through a cell has path/edge between 0 and 1.414; the cap clips lines with |offset| < 0.29 edge (up to ×1.41 too little mass); the mean over lines is 0.707 and is right. A plate at 45° meets 2220 kg/m² (×1.414), uncapped until cos θ = 0.04.
- Not exercised: every L4b line is within 0.01 rad of an axis (item 35). Proposed: decide whether a cell's chord may exceed 1 (the mass-on-path law says yes); one line in §13l.1.

### U1 (U) The plate's "five rounds" has no real anchor, and the physical reading is two
- Kernel depth of the 6 kg round in steel-strength plate: x = s₀/(c ρ) = 0.1293 m = 0.52 L = 2.07 calibres (`a1.mjs`), i.e. it stops 65 % through the 0.2 m plate.
- Same-spot repeat (the only thing a deterministic, dice-free loop does: B1 hits lie within 0.06 m of each other): the crater depths add; the second round meets 0.071 m of plate, r_remaining = 0.86e6 < s₀ = 1.57e6, and perforates. **Physical same-spot count: 2; the kernel: 4.6 → 5 (6 in the B6citadel run, 7 at T6, see T4).** The kernel's ÷ `hitsToWreck` turns "65 % of the thickness dug" into "21.5 % of the integrity".
- Absolute anchor: De Marre (T ∝ (m^0.5 v / d^0.75)^(1/0.7)): I could not find a K in a source I could cite (search found only the relative form and the Krupp K = 2400, which is another formula). **Only the scaling was checked**: De Marre gives penetration ∝ d^1.07 v^1.43 at constant L/D; the kernel gives d^1.0 v^1.0. Over 500–1000 m/s (the `muzzleVelocity` range) the kernel under-rewards speed (1000 vs 800: kernel +25 %, De Marre +37 %). My memory (not verified) is that a hardened AP round of this size defeats about 1–1.3 calibres of homogeneous plate at 800 m/s, so 2.07 calibres is generous to the round by about 2×.
- Proposed: treat "five" as a design number (the lever is `armour.plateThickness`, §13l.2); do not cite it as physics; if it must be physics, loss on a stopped round = depth fraction a / r (no ÷ hitsToWreck) and σ falls with integrity (L1).

### U2 (U) Beam on a plate: the hole the energy would melt is 0.28 m wide, and the deposit stays in the first 7 %
- 117.75 MJ at 1.2 MJ/kg (`data.js:89` note) melts 98 kg = 0.0125 m³ = a hole 0.28 m in diameter through 0.2 m. The plate's mean temperature rise is 6.7 K (117.75e6 / (39 250 × 450)), the "ruin" is 0.25 % of the plate melted (the note says so).
- The 100 MeV atoms (N = 2 bay, `beamEnergy` = 100.0 MeV at T1) stop in 113 kg/m² = 7 % of σ = 1570: all the deposit is in the first 1.4 cm, while the burn divides over the whole 39 t (the F1 resolution of #240). A ruined plate is a hole for every line across a 5 m segment, although the energy opened one channel: consistent only with the 1D line abstraction of the path model.
- Proposed: say in §13l.1 that a plate's ruin is "the energy that opens a channel of the beam's width through it" and that the hole generalises to the segment; no change.

### U3 (U) A plate's whole steel is coupled to its room's air instantly
- `rooms.js:1294-1306` `addPlate`: heat capacity = mass × 450 = 17.66e6 J/K added to the room; B6beam (`a10.mjs`): magazine behind 8 plates at **290.9 K** after 29.4 s of a 5 MW beam. Steel's diffusion time through 0.2 m is L²/α = 3140 s (α = 1.27e-5 m²/s, k = 45 W/m·K from memory), and in 30–100 s heat penetrates only 2.0–3.6 cm (√(α t)).
- The plates make the magazine thermally immune to the beam, on an anchor that is off by two orders (the plate would heat locally and the room almost not at all in 100 s). The consumer `thermal.coupledArmour` asked for in #248 Q13 is satisfied; its physics is the "whole mass" reading #240 (1) rejected for reactors.
- Proposed: couple a plate by its soak depth, mass × min(1, √(α t)/d) at the battle's time scale, or write the immunity down as a stand-in (E4 judges it).

## 2. The battle loop (`js/battle.js`)

Checked and sound: time of flight 900 m / 800 m/s = 1.125 s (B1: first hit at 1.1167 s); the segment test is a full Amanatides–Woo walk (`crossedCells`, no tunnelling at 13.3 m per step against 5 m cells); `speed = |r.ref.v − hull velocity at the entry point|` (`battle.js:340`, #242); the miss run (`a11.mjs`: plan.turretPoint off the ship, 6 shots, 4 misses, rounds culled) closes the linear book at **5.1e-15** of gross, so `cull`'s `left` book is exact between two ships.

### B1 (B) The impulse direction is not the round's relative velocity; the two-ship momentum book is open at 2e-5
- `js/battle.js:340-342`: `speed = len(rel)` from `rel = r.ref.v − pointVelocity(b, q)`, but `fireRound(..., direction: hit.dir)` with `hit.dir` the displacement direction of the round **in the body frame between two poses** (`lineHit`, `battle.js:247-249`). `fireRound` builds `u0 = hull + speed × dir` (`rooms.js:422`), so u0 ≠ the round's real velocity as soon as the hull spins: the frame turns by ω dt over the ~30 m between the centre and the entry point.
- Recomputed (`a4.mjs`, `a5.mjs`, B1 on R1): |u0 − round v| = 0.006 m/s with the target still, up to **0.107 m/s** (0.64 N·s of 4800) once ω reaches 8e-3 rad/s; summed over 12 hits, `battleMomentum(b)`: **|P − J| = 2.51 N·s = 2.2e-5 × gross (1.12e5)**; B6citadel 9.0e-6. The convention is 1e-9 × gross (#220). Not caught: `battleMomentum` (`battle.js:497`) is called by no scenario (grep over `mission0/`, `tools/`); `L4.route.split` and `L4.cull` book only one ship, with `external.J`.
- A passing round also leaves with `ref.v = u1` (`battle.js:352`), a velocity that was never its own to begin with: a jump of the same size.
- Proposed: `direction = rotate(rel/|rel|, −b.theta)` (the `rel` is already computed on the line above); add a scenario that sums `battleMomentum` over B1 at 1e-9.

### B2 (B) The impulse acts at the cell centre, not on the line: angular momentum is not conserved by a hit (the engineer's Q10)
- `js/rooms.js:431`: `receive(world, b, Ji, entryPoint(world, path[i]))` with `entryPoint` = the cell's centre (`rooms.js:316`); the round's line passes anywhere in the cell.
- `a2.mjs` (R1 bridge, a stopped round, 4800 N·s, line offset 0.5 → 4.5 m in the 5 m cell): the ship's torque is 16 149 N·m·s at every offset; the round's angular momentum before the hit is 6549 → 25 749. **Residual −9600 … +9600 N·m·s, zero only at the cell centre**; at most p × edge/2 = 12 000. In spin: 9600 / 2.7e8 (R1d's I) = 3.6e-5 rad/s per hit, 4 % of one recoil (0.050°/s, #236).
- Hidden at L4 because the turret aims at the cell centre (#267 (1)) and the target is static; opens at L5 with leading, dodge and scatter. `battleMomentum` has no L term and `ship.received` has no H, so nothing sums it: **not claimed, as the engineer said (Q10)**; `spent.L` is booked (`battle.js:349`) but unused.
- Proposed: apply each cell's J at the line's point in that cell (entry + t_mid × dir, already known to `lineHit` as `cells[k].t0/t1`), and add H to `received`.

### B3 (B, not claimed) Energy is not booked across two ships; a shot creates its energy
- No `battleEnergy` exists. At the shooter: the round leaves with 1.92 MJ + the recoil's kinetic energy; `turret.heatPerShot` books 0.3 MJ (`data.js:179`); `kinetic.chargeEnergy` (6 MJ per M round, `data.js:60`) is used only by the magazine's explosion. 6 − 1.92 − 0.3 = **3.78 MJ per shot unaccounted**; at 0.5 shots/s that is 1.9 MW, 38 % of a beam's 5 MW. The target side closes (F5, `rooms.js:443`; the checker's A3).
- Proposed: either book the unaccounted share as the turret's heat (the barrel and gas, a physical home for it) or state "the propellant's energy is not tracked" in §13l.1; add an energy total to `battleMomentum`'s sibling.

### B4 (B, small) A stopped round's mass leaves play
- `battle.js:348-350`: a stopped round's mass goes to `battle.spent.m` (72 kg for 12 rounds), its momentum is booked, but the hull's M, centre and I do not grow: 6.5e-5 of the target's mass. Momentum closes by construction; mass and inertia do not.
- Proposed: say so (negligible at T1; grows ∝ e³ with the round and with the ship alike, so the ratio is tier-invariant).

### L4 (L) The round's energy is split between cells by impulse, not by the energy each lost
- `js/rooms.js:443`: `heats[i] = heat × impulse_i / Σ impulse`. The round loses ½ m (v_in² − v_out²) in a cell, and v_in falls along the path.
- `a11.mjs`, six hold cells in a column: impulses 568 … 818 N·s (they grow as the round flattens); kernel heats 0.261 … 0.376 MJ; **sequential energy 0.427 … 0.153 MJ**, same sum 1.877 MJ. The first cell takes 0.427 MJ, the kernel books it 0.261; the last takes 0.153, the kernel 0.376.
- Consequence for item 34 below: the lead designer's "about 40 per cell" (#266) is the impulse split; with the sequential energy the first cell of the column is 21 rounds and the sixth 59.
- Proposed: when the energy rule is chosen, deposit the sequential energy; until then the heat split is harmless (heat is small).

## 3. The open physics for L5 (§17 items 32–36, #265, #266)

### (i) The passed cell's flat 1 / `hitsToWreck`

Recomputed with `fireRound` on one cell of each type (`a6.mjs`, M round, 800 m/s, T1; "energy rule" = E taken / (3000 J/kg × cell mass), the beam's law applied to the heat `fireRound` books):

| Cell | mass kg | σ kg/m² | s₀/r | E taken MJ | energy rule: loss per round | rounds | flat rule: rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hold / magazine | 3000 | 120 | 8.46 pass | 0.427 | 0.0475 | **21** | 3 |
| engine | 5700 | 228 | 4.45 pass | 0.766 | 0.0448 | 22 | 3 |
| beam bay | 5500 | 220 | 4.61 pass | 0.742 | 0.0450 | 22 | 3 |
| heat sink | 4290 | 172 | 5.91 pass | 0.594 | 0.0462 | 22 | 3 |
| shield generator | 10 000 | 400 | 2.54 pass | 1.215 | 0.0405 | 25 | 3 |
| bridge, quarters, turret | 14 800 | 592 | 1.71 pass | 1.587 | 0.0357 | 28 | 3 |
| reactor | 150 000 | 6000 | 0.17 **stops** | 1.920 | 0.0043 | **234** | 3 (17.7 by the stop's own rule, 0.056) |

**Why the counts are flat:** E taken ≈ r A v̄ ∝ σ, the ruin q × m ∝ σ edge², so loss per pass = c A v̄ / (q edge²) = 1545 × 3.06e-3 × 800 / (3000 × 25) = **0.0505 → 20 rounds**, independent of the cell's mass (and of the tier: A ∝ e², edge² ∝ e², c, v, q have k = 0). The flat 1/3 is 6.6× the energy rule for a hold cell and 7.8× for a bridge cell; the lead designer's observation holds for the kernel (21–28, not 40; his 40 came from the impulse split, L4). So under the energy rule **every passed cell is 20–30 rounds, the stopping cell (reactor) is 234**; a decapitation of R1 (four bridge cells, two per shot) would move from 12 shots to about 56 (arithmetic, not run), a duel 4–5× longer.

Physics, no real anchor found. The only mass-consistent *geometric* reading is the hole: A / edge² = 1.2e-4 of a cell's face, 8000 rounds; a round does not wreck a 25 m² compartment by its hole, so "1/3" and "1/20" are both functional abstractions (a room stops working long before its mass is gone) and the constant q = 3000 J/kg is calibrated on the beam (0.25 % melted), not on any impact data I could reach. I do not know a fetched value of the energy a steel structure absorbs per kilogram before it fails under impact; I did not search further.

### L2 (L) The flat loss is mass-blind and chord-blind, while its resistance is neither
- `js/rooms.js:415`: `(i < res.passed ? 1 : a / r[i]) / hits`. Resistance r = c σ chord (mass, geometry); the loss of a passed cell uses neither.
- Quote of the other side: `data.js:61` "a round passing a room cell takes 1 / this of its integrity" (D pass, #206), and `DESIGN.md` §17 item 34, which already names it.
- Proposed (for the pass of its own, #266): loss = E_i / (q m_i) with E_i the **sequential** energy (L4), per cell; counts above; or keep the flat rule as a stated abstraction and give the decision to the owner (Q2).

- A line along the corners of a diagonal (y = x + 0.05 m through R1's engine and heat sink, `a6.mjs`): chords 0.014, 1, 0.014, 1, 1, 0.014, 1 → 7 cells passed, **each loses 0.33**, sum 2.33 cells of integrity from one round, three of the cells for ≈1.4 % of their resistance. A round fired along a chain of corners wrecks the cells at the edge of the beam of its path.
- This is the "exploit for aim logic" of item 35, quantified. No L4b line is oblique, so no scenario sees it.
- Proposed: at least loss = chord / hitsToWreck for a passed cell (the minimal repair, chord is already known); better, the energy rule (L2).

### (ii) The magazine's detonation: a destroyed cell, or energy in its contents
- Today: `js/rooms.js:824-826` `ON_CELL_DESTROYED.magazine`: any destroyed cell with contents detonates all the contents (`magazineContents`: 150 HE rounds × (6 + 2) MJ = **1.2 GJ**).
- By energy in the contents: a round that passes a magazine cell leaves it ≤ 0.43 MJ (table). Into the ~900 kg of contents that is 1 K of bulk heating at 450 J/(kg·K) (0.3 K at a propellant's ~1.5 kJ/(kg·K), from memory); the contents' ignition is at hundreds of K. **A bulk energy criterion would never fire** (it takes 10⁵ passes). The question's second option is not a physical one.
- Real mechanism (qualitative; **I have no number I can cite**): shock or hot-spot initiation is a threshold in the impact speed and the size of what arrives (Held's v²d form for explosives; ordnance "insensitive munitions" tests), a local event, not an integral of deposited energy and not "the casing is destroyed". The kernel already has the quantity: the round's residual sectional momentum on reaching the contents (`penetrateRound` residual).
- Three rules, and what they do to the B6 counts (read from the hit log of `a9.mjs`; the third is my arithmetic, not a run): (a) today, destroyed cell: R1d 10 shots at T1; (b) energy in contents: never; (c) a round that passes a magazine cell's structure with residual speed above a threshold detonates it: the first round after the plate is gone, shot 7 of 10 (R1: shot 1–2 of 4).
- Proposed: (c) with a threshold in the round's speed on arrival (one new constant, `magazine.ignitionSpeed`, a lead-designer value), and a spalled fragment rule only if L5's debris needs it. Q1.

### (iii) The citadel's five rounds against a deliberate shot
- Kernel: the plate stops a round at 0.1293 m and loses 0.2154; the two hold cells in front reduce what reaches it (a = 1.198e6 → loss 0.165 while they stand), so the run is **6 stops at T1** (`a9.mjs`: shots 1–6 stop in the plate, shot 7 passes, the explosion at shot 10) and 7 at T6.
- Physical: same-spot crater accumulation, **2 rounds** (U1); with dispersion of a calibre or more at 900 m the plate takes hundreds. The game has no dispersion (#7, #240 (3)); a deterministic aim at one point is the same-spot case, so the plate is 2–3× thicker in the loop than the steel it is made of would be to a perfect gunner, and unboundedly thick to an imperfect one. The five is a number the aim error and the repair rate fix, not the plate.
- Proposed: record "citadel N rounds" as a design number set by `plateThickness`, `hitsToWreck` and the aim model together (L5's sensors and dispersion decide); do not rely on it as physics. U4.

### (iv) The grazed cell's full third
See L3. Physics: a graze takes the matter it crosses (chord) from the round and gives the cell the damage of that matter; the kernel already counts chord in resistance and drops it in damage.

### U4 (U) §17 item 32 rests on an aim model that does not exist yet
The five-round figure is exact under a deterministic perfect aim and says nothing about a dispersed one; item 33 (the information law) is its dependency. Recorded so the L5 plan does not read "5" as a cost to the enemy.
