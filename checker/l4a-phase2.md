## Discrepancies first (3)

1. **A plate is not burnt by a beam (physics, for the lead designer; not a scenario error).** `L0.armour.plate` asserts "the plate is not burnt" (`a.plate === 1`), following §13l.1, which names no burn for a plate. In phase 1 I derived a burn by the one law of #240 (1) / §13k.1 (a cell loses P F dt / (q m)): a 0.2 m plate (39 250 kg) would lose 7.077e-4 of its integrity per 5 MW step and be ruined in 1413 steps (23.5 s), while in the record the thin plate (d 0.01, 52 % of the beam) stays at integrity 1 and the hold cell behind it burns (0.99557 after one step = 0.4788 × 83 333 / 9e6, as I derive). As built, a plate of any thickness is immune to the beam, so R1c's citadel cannot be beamed open (L4b's B6 and the pace measure assume this). Either the rule gets a stated physical reason (it has none now: heat is booked, ruin is not) or plates burn by mass. The scenario matches the document; my derivation does not. The decision is not mine.
2. **`L3.shield.energy` criterion text vs its code.** The text says the ship takes J = 1920 N·s "at (52.66, 5) m" for g = 0.6; `expected()` (and the kernel, and §13k.1 "applied ... at the generator") use the generator's centre (20, 5): `dL = (20 − x_c) J` for both cases. Code and kernel are right and agree (heat 1 228 797.786764395 J). The text is wrong and, being in a locked criterion, cannot be edited; the record is correct. My own phase-1 g = 0.6 numbers (rotational gain 13.04 J, heat 1 228 785.02 J) put the impulse at the round's point and were wrong for the same reason: the correct rotational gain is 0.274 J.
3. **`L2.blast.integrity`, one-third case, sits on a threshold.** After `damageCell` the cells are at 0.33333333333333326, the hold capacity is 399 999.99999999994 and each cell's share 99 999.99999999999: the share equals the cell's remaining resistance, and "every cell destroyed" depends on the kernel's float snap (the record shows `destroyed: 4`). It passes deterministically, but §13l.4 / #177 say no case sits on a threshold unless it tests that threshold. The magazine's own four cells are also exactly at their resistance in every case (share 300 000 = R) and are neither checked nor recorded. Low severity; a 0.3 instead of 1/3 would keep the physics and drop the knife edge.

## Tolerances

Closed forms 1e-12 are sized right (float noise of a few 1e-16 per operation; the largest recorded residue is 1.1e-12 relative in `vented`, which is why that check uses 1e-9). Timed cases are one-sided in [t, t + dt) off the grid (7542.86 and 15 085.7 steps). Books 1e-9 × gross hold with a large margin (dP residue 1.9e-12 against 24 000). The csda tolerance is the one with a wide margin (50×), stated above.
