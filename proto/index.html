    Battle the phases · both captains the standard enemy

    Both ships are flown by the same automatic captain (js/enemy.js, standard personality), which picks Fire, Cool or Evade itself. You start on Stand off: weapons held until contact is over. Then you choose from your build's menu (right panel) at any time; a decision point asks you at contact's end and when you are hit while attacking. Pause: the battle waits for your choice; Auto: the automatic choice applies and you may change it. Your verbs: scan a zone, target a known room. The seed draws the dodge intervals; Restart adds 1.
